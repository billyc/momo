      • About MoMo
      • Modeling Mobility Conference: Code of Conduct

      MoMo brings together the community working at the intersection of transportation modeling, data, and planning to connect, share ideas, and advance the practice. We are excited to welcome practitioners, researchers, students, and innovators to Tempe in Spring 2027

      Overview Plan Registration Get involved Sponsorship Agenda

      The theme for this conference is converge. Momo is the forum where ideas, tools, and perspectives converge to address complex mobility challenges. It is also the space where transportation professionals from public agencies, private consultants and academic institutes converge to shape the future of modeling mobility. After all, as transportation modelers, our endgame is always convergence.

      Get the latest updates by signing up for the momo 2027 mailing list.

      Propose a session

      Do you have an idea for a session? Submit your ideas using the instructions below.

      Potential Session Types

      • Panel
      • Workshop
      • Hackathon
      • User Group

      We’re looking for sessions that spark discussion, challenge assumptions, showcase multiple perspectives, or help attendees tackle problems together. Examples from momo 2025 include the Exploring Post-pandemic modeling panel, the General Modeling Network Specification (GMNS) workshop, and the ActivitySim user group session.

      Submission Requirements

      Description / Speakers

      • Session Title: Short, clear, and descriptive (100-character maximum).
      • Session Organizer(s): Name, title, affiliation, and contact email.
      • Invited Speakers: Names and proposed topics of confirmed or prospective presenters.
      • Session Abstract: Up to 500 words summarizing the problem, proposed approach, and relevance to practitioners.

      Structure

      • Session organization
      • Anticipated room / material / AV requirements

      Audience

      • Target Audience & Technical Level: Indicate beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
      • Actionable Takeaways: 2–3 concrete things attendees will learn or leave with.

      Check back soon for submittal instructions.

      Disclaimer

      Submissions may receive input from the planning committee regarding their participants and scope to ensure they align with the theme, intent, and program of the conference. The committee is committed to a collaborative process with submitters to modify sessions so they effectively address all requirements.
